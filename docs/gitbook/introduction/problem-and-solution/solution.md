# Solution

ProofBill connects a funded permission, a private delivery, and a confirmed payment. The owner controls the budget and assignment; the application checks evidence; the contract applies the financial policy.

## Bound the permission

A pass records its owner, agent, fixed service/seller, expiry, remaining budget, payment policy, and optional purchase ceiling. Contract checks reject stale authorization and purchases that cannot fit the allocation. The current service price is 0.010 testnet NEAR. The optional ceiling caps that quote; it does not set the seller’s price.

## Keep purchase content behind buyer access

Saved briefs and results are encrypted and bound to their historical buyer/order. Onchain commitments link those private bytes to public financial facts. A signed buyer session opens the result; a public receipt omits the private delivery bundle.

## Choose the payment policy

Manual requires buyer approval after delivery. Automatic uses advance authorization after matching publisher delivery. Both become Paid only after a successful seller-transfer callback. Neither evidence nor payment establishes answer quality.

## Reuse the remaining entitlement

The holder can buy again, transfer an available pass, or close it with an eligible service refund. Transfer clears the old agent/Automatic permission; previous private results stay with their historical buyers.

The NFT provides standard ownership and transfer representation. It is not an autonomous wallet. A conventional transferable contract record could implement similar financial rules; marketplace demand and the usefulness of NFT handover still need testing.
